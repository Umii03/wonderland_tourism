Important Travel Guidelines

Required Documents

Every member must keep their original CNIC during the trip.

Safety & Legal Compliance

Illegal drugs, narcotics, weapons, or prohibited items are strictly not allowed. Participants are personally responsible for any illegal items found in their possession.

Respectful Conduct

Physical harm, verbal abuse, harassment, humiliation, immoral conduct, or misbehavior with travelers, organizers, or team members is strictly prohibited.

Privacy & Photography

Travelers must take permission before photographing other participants, especially female travelers. Privacy and respect are mandatory.

Trip Changes & Delays

Delays may occur due to traffic, weather, landsliding, road conditions, hotel availability, peak seasons, public holidays, mechanical issues, or other circumstances beyond company control.

Additional Expenses

Participants may be responsible for extra expenses such as secondary transport, additional meals, extra hotel charges, transport rent, or extensions caused by delays or unforeseen circumstances.

Adventure Travel Risk

Outdoor and adventure activities may carry potential risks. Participants should travel responsibly and follow organizer instructions.

Right to Cancel or Remove Participants

The company reserves the right to cancel trips before or during travel. In case of serious misconduct or rule violation, organizers may remove a participant from the trip without refund.
